# Naming: a quit-nicotine system

Covers cigarettes, vapes and e-cigarettes.

Positioning the name has to carry:

- **For** people who have already tried to quit by willpower and failed, **ВЫДОХ is** a 21-day
  system that plans for the minute the craving actually hits — whether the nicotine comes from
  a cigarette, a vape or an e-cigarette.
- **Unlike** a willpower app, a nicotine patch alone, or a lecture from a doctor, **it** gives you
  one thing to do at 14:40 on a Tuesday: breathe, wait out the wave, and see what the habit costs
  and what quitting pays back.

## Shortlist

| Name | Territory | Why it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ВЫДОХ** | Evocative | The exhale is the body's own relief action; it is what you do instead of smoking. Short, sayable, ownable, and it fits the breath-arc motif. | Could be read as "exhaling smoke"; the site flips it in the first screen. Domain unverified. |
| **ПЕРЕДЫШКА** | Evocative | "A breather" — the pause between cigarettes, reframed as a break you keep. Warm and human. | Longer, harder at small sizes. |
| **ВОЗДУХ** | Descriptive / evocative | Literal air. Instantly understood. | Generic; crowded category; weak trademark. |
| **НЕ КУРЮ** | Descriptive | The outcome said out loud. Good for a community. | Sounds like a slogan, not a brand; hard to own. |
| **ТИХО** | Evocative | Quiet — the opposite of the alarm of a health warning. | Does not say what it does. |

## Recommendation

**ВЫДОХ.** It is one word, it is a physical action the visitor can take immediately (the breathing
tool), and it gives the whole site a motif that is not a cigarette, a lung illustration or a red
cross. It also sets up the closing line: «Сделай последний выдох дыма».

**Before committing:** check trademark availability in the relevant classes (health / mobile
software) and the .ru / .com domains. Neither was verified here.
